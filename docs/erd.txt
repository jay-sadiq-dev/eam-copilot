Relationships
-------------
- task_plans 1 -> N tasks         (tasks.task_plan_id)
- pm_schedules 1 -> N pm_lines    (pm_lines.pm_schedule_id)
- tppm has foreign keys to task_plans (task_plan_id) and pm_schedules (pm_schedule_id),
  and to users (created_by, confirmed_by) to record who ran the check and who confirmed the link.
- task_plans, pm_schedules and users are independent of each other.


The idea
--------
- A functional consultant uploads an OEM manual. The AI drafts a task plan (what to do)
  and a PM schedule (when to do it) as two separate documents.
- tasks and pm_lines are the rows inside those documents, so each lives in its own child table.
- An admin approves or rejects task plans and PM schedules only (status on each document).
  Admins never approve a TPPM.
- tppm links one approved task plan to one approved PM schedule, after four checks:
  both approved, same asset, referenced tasks exist, all tasks scheduled.
  Pass -> proposed link the consultant confirms. Fail -> saved as failed, with reasons.
- pm_lines.task_numbers refers to tasks.n by value, with no foreign key on purpose:
  the schedule is drafted independently, so the "tasks exist" check must be able to fail.
- The AI drafts, checks and proposes; humans approve and confirm.
